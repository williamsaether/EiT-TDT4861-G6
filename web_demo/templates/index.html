Fusion Weights

Weather & Camera are additive blend weights (normalized to sum to 1). Conf. Sensitivity controls how much low model confidence damps the camera reading toward a safe neutral — set to 0 to always trust the raw camera factor regardless of confidence.

Camera Averaging Window

Predictions from the last 3.0 s are averaged before computing the camera factor. A longer window smooths jitter but reacts more slowly to sudden surface changes.
